This is part 3 of a three-part series, written in my junior year of high school, that works up to proving that (Z/pZ)<sup>×</sup> is cyclic: the multiplicative group of integers mod a prime has a generator. I was teaching myself group theory at the time and worked out the route on my own, so the proofs are mine rather than the textbook's.

This part puts the pieces together: the multiplicative group of any finite field is cyclic, and its additive group is a vector space over Z/pZ.
