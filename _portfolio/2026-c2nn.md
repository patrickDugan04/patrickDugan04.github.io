---
title: "C2NN: a C-to-neural-network compiler"
excerpt: "An LLVM-based compiler that lowers C programs into equivalent neural networks."
collection: portfolio
date: 2026-06-01
---

C2NN lowers C programs into neural networks that compute the same function. It translates control flow and arithmetic over LLVM IR into differentiable network structure. The goal is to initialize a model's weights from existing code, so training starts from a working program rather than from noise.

**Stack:** LLVM, C++, PyTorch. In progress.
